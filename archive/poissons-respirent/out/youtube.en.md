How do fish breathe underwater? 🐟 The Little Explorers

## Description
How do fish breathe underwater? 🐟 The answer in one minute, with Noah, Mila and their lab vehicle Curio.

Remember: Fish catch the oxygen hidden in the water with their gills.

🧪 Try it at home, with an adult: in front of an aquarium, count how many times the fish opens its mouth in one minute, and watch its gill slits move!

Every day, a real question from a child and a real scientific answer, checked before it is told. For children aged 5 to 9, and for parents who have run out of answers.
