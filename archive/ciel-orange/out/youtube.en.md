## Description
Why does the sky turn orange in the evening? 🌅 The answer in one minute, with Noah, Mila and their lab vehicle Curio.

Remember: In the evening, blue gets scattered on the way: orange is left.

🧪 Try it at home, with an adult: look at the lamp through the glass of water and milk. It looks orange, like the evening Sun!

Every day, a real question from a child and a real scientific answer, checked before it is told. For children aged 5 to 9, and for parents who have run out of answers.
