Why do volcanoes erupt? 🌋 The Little Explorers

## Description
Why do volcanoes erupt? 🌋 The answer in one minute, with Noah, Mila and their lab vehicle Curio.

Remember: Magma rises because it is lighter, and its gas bubbles push it out.

🧪 Try it at home, with an adult: outside, shake a bottle of soda and open it. The bubbles push the soda out, just like gas pushes the magma!

Every day, a real question from a child and a real scientific answer, checked before it is told. For children aged 5 to 9, and for parents who have run out of answers.
