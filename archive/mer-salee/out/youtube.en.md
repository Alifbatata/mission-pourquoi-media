Why is the sea salty? 🌊 The Little Explorers

## Description
Why is the sea salty? 🌊 The answer in one minute, with Noah, Mila and their lab vehicle Curio.

Remember: Rain wears down rocks, rivers carry the salt to the sea, and the water leaves without it.

🧪 Try it at home, with an adult: put very salty water in a plate, in the sun. When the water is gone… the salt is still there!

Every day, a real question from a child and a real scientific answer, checked before it is told. For children aged 5 to 9, and for parents who have run out of answers.
