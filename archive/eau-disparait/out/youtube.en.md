## Description
Where does water go when it disappears? 💧 The answer in one minute, with Noah, Mila and their lab vehicle Curio.

Remember: Water doesn't disappear: it flies off into the air, invisible, and will come back as rain.

🧪 Try it at home, with an adult: two glasses of water, a marker line. One in the sun, one in the shade. Compare in two days!

Every day, a real question from a child and a real scientific answer, checked before it is told. For children aged 5 to 9, and for parents who have run out of answers.
