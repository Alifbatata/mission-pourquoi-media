## Description
Why is there day, then night? 🌍 The answer in one minute, with Noah, Mila and their lab vehicle Curio.

Remember: The Earth spins on itself: when your side faces the Sun, it's daytime.

🧪 Try it at home, with an adult: in the dark, a lamp and an orange on a pencil. Stick on a dot, turn the orange: day… night… day!

Every day, a real question from a child and a real scientific answer, checked before it is told. For children aged 5 to 9, and for parents who have run out of answers.
