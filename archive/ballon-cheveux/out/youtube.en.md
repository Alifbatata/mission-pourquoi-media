## Description
Why does hair stand up with a balloon? 🎈 The answer in one minute, with Noah, Mila and their lab vehicle Curio.

Remember: Rubbing moves tiny charges: like ones push apart, opposite ones pull together.

🧪 Try it at home, with an adult: rub a balloon on your hair, then bring it close to little bits of paper. They jump onto it!

Every day, a real question from a child and a real scientific answer, checked before it is told. For children aged 5 to 9, and for parents who have run out of answers.
