Why does popcorn pop? 🍿 The Little Explorers

## Description
Why does popcorn pop? 🍿 The answer in one minute, with Noah, Mila and their lab vehicle Curio.

Remember: The water in the kernel turns to steam, the pressure cracks the shell, and the starch puffs up into foam.

🧪 Try it at home, with an adult: watch the kernels through a glass lid. Count the pops… and look for the hard shell on each flake!

Every day, a real question from a child and a real scientific answer, checked before it is told. For children aged 5 to 9, and for parents who have run out of answers.
