## Description
Why do leaves change color in autumn? 🍂 The answer in one minute, with Noah, Mila and their lab vehicle Curio.

Remember: The green disappears and reveals the hidden yellow; the red, though, is made in autumn.

🧪 Try it at home, with an adult: collect leaves and line them up from the greenest to the reddest. Which one still has a little green?

Every day, a real question from a child and a real scientific answer, checked before it is told. For children aged 5 to 9, and for parents who have run out of answers.
