How does sound travel to our ears? 👂 The Little Explorers

## Description
How does sound travel to our ears? 👂 The answer in one minute, with Noah, Mila and their lab vehicle Curio.

Remember: Whatever makes a sound vibrates; the air passes the vibration along, bit by bit, to your eardrum.

🧪 Try it at home, with an adult: make the end of a ruler vibrate where it sticks out over the table. The shorter it is, the higher the pitch!

Every day, a real question from a child and a real scientific answer, checked before it is told. For children aged 5 to 9, and for parents who have run out of answers.
