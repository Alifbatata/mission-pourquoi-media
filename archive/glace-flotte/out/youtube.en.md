Why does ice float on water? 🧊 The Little Explorers

## Description
Why does ice float on water? 🧊 The answer in one minute, with Noah, Mila and their lab vehicle Curio.

Remember: When water freezes, it takes up more room: ice is lighter than water, so it floats.

🧪 Try it at home, with an adult: fill a small bottle with water to the brim and put it in the freezer. Tomorrow, the ice will have swollen the bottle!

Every day, a real question from a child and a real scientific answer, checked before it is told. For children aged 5 to 9, and for parents who have run out of answers.
