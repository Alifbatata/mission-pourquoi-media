How does a rainbow form? 🌈 The Little Explorers

## Description
How does a rainbow form? 🌈 The answer in one minute, with Noah, Mila and their lab vehicle Curio.

Remember: Each raindrop splits the sunlight into colors and sends them back to you.

🧪 Try it at home, with an adult: on a very sunny day, put the Sun behind you and make a fine spray with the garden hose. Your very own rainbow!

Every day, a real question from a child and a real scientific answer, checked before it is told. For children aged 5 to 9, and for parents who have run out of answers.
